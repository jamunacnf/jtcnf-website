How to Import Goods Without a Local Presence

October 15, 2024 By Admin Business

If you are an international company looking to sell products in Bangladesh but do not have a registered legal entity here, you might think importing is impossible. Fortunately, the concept of an Importer of Record (IOR) solves this problem.

What is an Importer of Record (IOR)?

An IOR is an individual or company that is legally responsible for importing goods into a country. They handle all the customs documentation, pay the necessary duties, and ensure compliance with local laws on behalf of the actual owner of the goods.

How it Works

As an IOR, M/S. Jamuna Traders can act as the official importer for your shipment. Once the goods clear customs, we transfer ownership to your local distributor or buyer. This allows you to do business in Bangladesh without setting up a costly local office.

  • We handle the Bill of Entry under our name.
  • We ensure all regulatory compliance is met.
  • You maintain control over your product and supply chain.
