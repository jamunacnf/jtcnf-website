Who We Are

At M/S. Jamuna Traders, we are dedicated to providing reliable, efficient, and professional Customs Clearing and Forwarding services to importers and exporters in Bangladesh.

With our primary operational bases at Dhaka Airport and Chattogram Port, we ensure that your cargo moves smoothly through customs, avoiding unnecessary delays and complications.

We understand that every shipment is critical to your business. That is why our team of experienced professionals works tirelessly to handle all documentation, customs regulations, and freight coordination, giving you peace of mind.
